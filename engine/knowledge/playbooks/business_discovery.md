# Business discovery

## Listen
- Clarify the decision, audience, current situation, evidence, constraints, and what is out of scope.
- Separate observed facts from assumptions and questions that need validation.

## Shape
- Summarize the opportunity and its likely customer value.
- Compare a small number of options, identify risks, and define low-cost validation.

## Deliver
- Set out a short action plan with owners, milestones, evidence to collect, and review points.
- Treat forecasts as hypotheses, not guarantees; ask a human to approve consequential decisions.
