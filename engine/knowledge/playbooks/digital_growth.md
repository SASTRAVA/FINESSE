# Digital growth

## Listen
- Define audience, offer, geography, current channels, business goals, and available analytics.
- Distinguish verified customer insight from assumptions.

## Shape
- Prioritize search intent, useful content, and channels against effort and measurable outcomes.
- Set a baseline and avoid unsupported performance or ranking guarantees.

## Deliver
- Create a sequenced content and optimization plan with owners, cadence, KPIs, and review checkpoints.
- Keep claims accurate and obtain approval before publishing or contacting prospects.
