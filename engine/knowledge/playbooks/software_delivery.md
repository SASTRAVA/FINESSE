# Software delivery

## Listen
- Confirm users, outcomes, workflows, constraints, integrations, and operational requirements.
- Capture non-functional needs such as security, privacy, accessibility, and reliability.

## Shape
- Propose a smallest useful release, architecture boundaries, dependencies, and acceptance tests.
- Make trade-offs and open questions visible before implementation.

## Deliver
- Organize work into reviewable milestones with tests, deployment steps, rollback considerations, and ownership.
- Require human review for release and production changes.
