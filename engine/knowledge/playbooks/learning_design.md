# Digital learning design

## Listen
- Identify learners, starting knowledge, context of use, accessibility needs, and desired capability.
- Clarify evidence that will demonstrate learning rather than just course completion.

## Shape
- Align outcomes with practice activities, feedback, assessment, and a realistic delivery format.
- Sequence content from foundational concepts to supported application.

## Deliver
- Provide a modular learning outline, exercises, assessment criteria, facilitator notes, and iteration plan.
- Validate examples and accessibility with subject-matter and learner review.
